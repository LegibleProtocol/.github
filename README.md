# Legible Protocol

An open standard for expanding the universe of intelligence that can be made legible and coordinated.

A contribution can emerge before a project, a team or a compensation
agreement exists. Legible offers a common grammar for preserving these
formulations, relating them to other work and acknowledging consequences
received from their use.

The aim is to expand what can be found, understood and considered,
including contributions that were not initially selected. Its records
can move between tools and contexts, supporting interpretation by people
and AI systems.

## A grammar for contributions

- Appoint: declares a formulation, such as a problem, a hypothesis,
  a criterion or a solution.
- Trace: declares dependency relationships between contributions.
- Nod: acknowledges a consequence already received from a contribution.

This shared foundation helps readers examine contributions and their
relationships. Those using the records decide how to consider them
and what coordination arrangements to build.

## Explore and contribute

The project includes a draft specification and experimental implementations,
open to review and practical trials.

You can contribute questions, critiques, experience reports, documentation,
translations or code. Each repository provides its licenses and contribution
guidelines.
